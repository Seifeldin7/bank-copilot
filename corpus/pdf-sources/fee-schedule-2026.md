# Retail Fee Schedule 2026
Synthetic training material. Not an official policy of any bank.
Document owner: Retail Banking Product Management
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01

## Purpose and how to use this schedule

This schedule lists the standard fees that the Bank charges retail customers for cards, account services, transfers, ATM use and digital services during 2026. Branch employees use it to answer customer questions about the cost of a service before the service is requested.

All amounts are in Egyptian pounds (EGP) and are per item unless the line says otherwise. Fees are debited from the linked account of the customer after the related service request has been approved by a supervisor. Fees are never collected in cash at the counter.

## Rules for employees

- Quote fees only from this schedule. Do not quote from memory or from older notices.
- Tell the customer the fee before the request is submitted and note in the request description that it was explained.
- Employees cannot waive, reduce or change a fee. A waiver request must be raised as a service request and needs supervisor approval.
- Free services are labelled free in this schedule. If a service is not listed, ask your supervisor before giving an answer.

## Layout of the schedule

- Page 2: Card fees.

## Fee changes

The Bank may change its fees with advance notice to customers. A new version of this schedule will be published when this happens. Until then, the fees on the following pages apply. If a customer asks about a fee that is different from what is written here, check the version number and the effective date at the top of the document and inform your supervisor.

## Card fees

Debit card issuance is free for the first card. Card replacement costs EGP 50. Annual fees for classic credit cards are EGP 300.

The lines below set out the card fees for retail customers.

## Debit card fees

- Debit card issuance, first card: free
- Debit card issuance, additional card: EGP 75
- Card replacement (damaged or design change): EGP 50
- Card replacement (bank-side defect or expiry): free
- Card PIN re-issue: EGP 20

The EGP 50 fee applies to damaged cards and to design changes. It does not apply when the card has a bank-side defect or when the card has expired or expires within the next 30 days. Lost card replacement follows the same fee line as other replacements and also needs supervisor approval before the card is issued.

## Credit card fees

- Classic credit card, annual fee: EGP 300
- Gold credit card, annual fee: EGP 600
- Platinum credit card, annual fee: EGP 1,200
- Supplementary credit card, annual fee: EGP 150
- Credit card replacement (damaged): EGP 75
- Late payment fee: EGP 150

A limit increase request carries no separate fee. See the Credit Card Limits and Limit Inquiries guide for the documents that are needed.

## How card fees are charged

1. The fee is shown to the customer when the request is created.
2. The supervisor approves the request.
3. The system debits the fee from the linked account.
4. The system records the debit in the request history.

## Customer questions

If a customer asks why the first debit card is free but a second card is not, explain that the first card is part of the standard account package and additional cards are a separate product. If the customer disputes a fee that was already charged, record a complaint and do not reverse the fee yourself.

## Account service fees

Balance certificates cost EGP 25 each. Archived statements older than 5 years cost EGP 100 per request. Cheque books of 25 leaves cost EGP 40.

The lines below list the fees for common account services requested at the branch.

## Certificates and statements

- Balance certificate: EGP 25
- Account statement, current period up to 12 months: free
- Archived statement older than 5 years: EGP 100 per request
- Duplicate statement copy: EGP 10 per copy
- Account confirmation letter: EGP 30

A balance certificate is issued on official letterhead and is ready the next working day. Statements for any period up to 12 months per request are free. Statements older than 5 years are retrieved from the archive and can take up to 10 working days.

## Cheque books

- Cheque book, 25 leaves: EGP 40
- Cheque book, 50 leaves: EGP 75
- Replacement of a lost cheque book: EGP 60
- Stop payment on a cheque: EGP 50

Cheque books are issued only for active current accounts. Savings accounts are not eligible. Cheque books are printed centrally and delivered to the requested branch within 7 working days.

## Other account services

- Account closure: free
- Signature card update: free
- Dormant account reactivation: free
- Standing order set-up: EGP 20

## Notes for employees

- Check the account type before quoting a cheque book fee.
- A customer who needs a balance certificate for an embassy or visa application should be told that the certificate is ready the next working day.
- The fee is debited after the supervisor approves the request.
- If the account balance is not enough on the day of approval, the branch contacts the customer and the service is not completed until the fee is collected.

## Transfer and ATM fees

This page lists the fees for payments, transfers and cash withdrawals. Fees on this page are charged automatically by the system and do not need a branch request, unless a customer asks for a manual transfer to be prepared at the counter.

## Transfers

- Transfer between own accounts at the Bank: free
- Transfer to another customer of the Bank: free
- Transfer to an account at another bank through ACH: EGP 10
- Instant transfer to an account at another bank: EGP 15
- Transfer prepared at the branch counter: EGP 25
- International transfer, outgoing: EGP 150

Transfers to accounts at other banks through ACH may take one working day. Instant transfers are processed within minutes, subject to the receiving bank.

## ATM fees

- Cash withdrawal at an ATM of the Bank: free
- Cash withdrawal at an ATM of another bank in Egypt: EGP 6
- Balance inquiry at an ATM of another bank: EGP 2
- Cash withdrawal abroad: EGP 60
- Mini statement at an ATM of the Bank: free

## Returned items

- Returned cheque, insufficient funds: EGP 100
- Returned standing order, insufficient funds: EGP 30

A customer with a returned cheque in the last six months is not eligible for a new cheque book.

## Foreign currency fees

- Foreign currency account opening: free
- Foreign currency cash withdrawal above USD 10,000 or equivalent: EGP 100 and 48 hours notice to the branch

## Notes for employees

- Explain the difference between ACH and instant transfers when a customer asks about speed.
- Do not promise delivery times beyond those stated here.
- If a customer questions an ATM fee charged by another bank, explain that the Bank does not control fees of other banks.

## Digital, alert and maintenance fees

This page covers digital banking services, alerts and recurring account maintenance fees, followed by general notes for employees.

## Digital banking fees

- Internet banking enrollment: free
- Mobile banking app: free
- One-time password by SMS: free
- Unlock of a locked digital profile: free
- New activation code request: free

Internet banking activation codes expire after 24 hours. A new code is free of charge.

## Alert fees

- SMS alerts for account activity: EGP 5 per month
- SMS alerts for card transactions: EGP 5 per month
- Email alerts: free
- Combined SMS alerts package: EGP 8 per month

## Account maintenance fees

- Current account maintenance: EGP 15 per month
- Savings account maintenance: free
- Foreign currency account maintenance: EGP 10 per month
- Minor account maintenance: free
- Account below the minimum balance: EGP 25 per month

## General notes

1. All fees are shown in EGP and may be subject to applicable taxes.
2. Fees are debited from the linked account only after the related request has been approved.
3. All service requests need supervisor approval before execution.
4. Fees cannot be changed by branch employees.
5. Customers who dispute a fee should be offered the complaints procedure, and the complaint must be recorded on the day it is received.
