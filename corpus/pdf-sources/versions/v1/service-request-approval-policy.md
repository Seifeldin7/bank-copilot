# Service Request Approval Policy
Synthetic training material. Not an official policy of any bank.
Document owner: Branch Operations and Governance Department
Version: 1
Effective date: 2025-02-01
## Purpose and scope
This policy describes how service requests raised in branches of the Bank are approved before they are carried out. A service request is a formal instruction to change something for a customer, for example ordering a replacement card, resetting a digital banking profile, issuing a cheque book or a statement, or asking a customer to supply missing documents.

The policy applies to all branch employees. It aims to keep customer changes accurate and traceable while keeping everyday service quick. This is version 1 of the policy. It may be replaced by a later version, and employees must always check which version is in force.

## Definitions
- Employee: any member of branch staff who prepares a request.
- Senior employee: an employee with at least two years of service who has been granted senior status by the branch manager.
- Supervisor: a team leader appointed by the branch manager with authority over a section of the branch.
- Request: the record that describes the action, the customer reference (for example CUS-123456), and any supporting details.

## Request categories
Requests belong to one of four categories:
1. Card service, such as card replacement or limit enquiry.
2. Digital banking, such as password reset or device change.
3. Account service, such as cheque books and statements.
4. Additional documentation, such as asking for missing documents.

## General rules
- Every request must record the customer reference and the last four digits of any card or account. Full numbers must never be written in notes.
- The employee preparing the request must check the customer identity before submitting it.
- A request that is incomplete must be corrected by the employee who prepared it.
- The person who approves a request decides whether the information is sufficient to proceed.

## Approval thresholds
Service requests may be approved by any senior employee. Supervisor approval is required only for card replacements.

This means that most requests, including digital banking requests, account service requests and additional documentation requests, can be approved by a senior employee on duty. A supervisor does not need to be involved unless the request is a card replacement. Card replacements include replacements of damaged, lost, stolen and expired cards and replacements requested because of a design change.

## Practical examples
- A cheque book request for a current account is approved by a senior employee.
- A request to remind a customer to bring missing documents is approved by a senior employee.
- A mobile banking device change is approved by a senior employee.
- A replacement of a lost debit card must be approved by a supervisor.

## Choosing the approver
1. Identify the category of the request.
2. If the request is a card replacement, send it to a supervisor.
3. For every other category, send it to any available senior employee.
4. If no senior employee is available, wait for one or ask the branch manager to nominate one.

## Approver duties
The approver must read the request, check the customer reference, and confirm that the information is complete. The approver may ask the employee to add detail but should avoid long delays, because customers are usually waiting at the counter.

An approval is recorded with the name of the approver and the date. Approvals given verbally without a record are not valid.

## Execution
After approval, the employee carries out the action in the relevant system and records the result in the request. The execution record should show the date, the employee who executed it, and any reference produced by the system, such as a card order reference.

Approval authority under this version is shared across the branch, so branch managers should make sure that senior status is given only to employees who understand the rules of each category.

## Request lifecycle
A request moves through simple stages. It starts as a draft while the employee prepares it. When the employee is ready, the request is submitted for approval. The approver then approves or rejects it. An approved request is carried out by the employee and then marked as done.

The employee should keep the request open until the action is complete, and should tell the customer what to expect, including the expected delivery time for cards.

## Rejection
An approver who does not approve a request should return it to the employee with a short comment. A comment is encouraged but this version of the policy does not set a strict format. The employee may correct the request and submit it again.

## Corrections
If the approver notices a small mistake, such as a typing error in a branch code, the approver may ask the employee to fix it or may fix it directly. Changes are usually noted in the request comments.

## Handling urgent requests
Urgent requests, for example the replacement of a card reported stolen, should be passed to the supervisor immediately. Because the supervisor is the only approver for card replacements, the branch manager must ensure at least one supervisor is available during opening hours.

If no supervisor is present, the branch manager may approve a card replacement personally and must record this in the request.

## Customer communication
The employee tells the customer the reference of the request and the expected time to complete it. Employees must not promise outcomes before the request is approved. If a request is declined, the employee explains the reason in plain language, without disclosing internal comments.

## Records and review
Requests and approvals are kept for at least five years. They may be reviewed by the branch manager, internal audit and the compliance unit.

Each month, the branch manager samples a number of approved requests and checks that:
- The customer reference was recorded correctly.
- Card replacements were approved by a supervisor.
- Other requests were approved by a senior employee.
- Execution was recorded and no action was done twice.

## Responsibilities
- Employees prepare requests accurately and protect customer data.
- Senior employees approve non-card requests carefully and without delay.
- Supervisors approve all card replacements.
- Branch managers appoint senior employees and supervisors and monitor compliance.

## Use of tools
Employees may use the branch systems to prepare and submit requests. Any additional tool that helps draft text must not be given access to customer data beyond what the employee is allowed to see.

## Exceptions
Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Branch Operations and Governance Department. The branch manager may not grant an exception on their own.

## Review of this policy
This policy is reviewed every year. The department may update the approval thresholds if audit reviews show that risks have increased. Employees will be told of any new version before it takes effect, and the previous version stops being valid on the effective date of the new version.

## Document control
This document is a historical record. It is kept so that staff can understand how earlier requests were approved and so that auditors can compare past decisions with the rules in force at the time.

Version 1 is the first issued version of this policy. Questions should be sent to the Branch Operations and Governance Department through the branch manager.
