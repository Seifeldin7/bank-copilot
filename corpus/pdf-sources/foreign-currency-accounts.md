# Foreign Currency Accounts
Synthetic training material. Not an official policy of any bank.
Document owner: Retail Accounts Department
Version: 1
Effective date: 2025-03-01
## Purpose and scope
This document explains how branch employees of the Bank open, maintain and service foreign currency accounts for retail customers. It is written for tellers, customer service officers and senior employees who handle account questions at the counter.
A foreign currency account is a deposit account that holds funds in a currency other than the Egyptian pound (EGP). Customers use these accounts to keep savings in a stable foreign currency, to receive remittances from abroad, and to pay for travel or study costs that are billed in foreign currency.

This document covers the following topics:
- Which currencies the Bank supports and the minimum opening balance.
- How a new account is opened and which documents are required.
- Rules for deposits and cash withdrawals, including the notice requirement for large withdrawals.
- Statements, dormancy and account closure.

## Who may open an account
Any customer who already holds an active EGP account with the Bank, and who has completed identity verification, may open a foreign currency account. A joint account may be opened only if every account holder is verified in person at the branch.

Minors and customers whose identity records are incomplete cannot open a foreign currency account. If a customer is not eligible, the employee must explain the reason politely and offer to start the missing verification steps.

## General principles
Employees must follow these principles whenever they discuss a foreign currency account with a customer:
1. Quote only the currencies and conditions listed in this document.
2. Never promise an exchange rate. Rates are published by the treasury unit and change during the day.
3. Never write a full account number in notes. Record only the last four digits of the account, for example ending 4821.
4. Refer any question about taxes or foreign regulations to the customer's own adviser.

When a customer asks for something this document does not cover, the employee should ask a senior employee instead of guessing. Branch staff may prepare an account service request for the customer, but the request follows the Service Request Approval Policy that is currently in force.

## Supported currencies
Foreign currency accounts are available in US dollars, euros, and pounds sterling. The minimum opening balance is 100 units of the account currency. For example, a customer opening a US dollar account must deposit at least USD 100, and a customer opening a euro account must deposit at least EUR 100.

The Bank does not offer foreign currency accounts in any other currency. If a customer asks for a different currency, the employee should explain that only the three currencies above are available and suggest the closest alternative.

Currency codes used in branch systems are:
- USD for US dollars.
- EUR for euros.
- GBP for pounds sterling.

## Opening balance rules
The minimum opening balance must be deposited when the account is opened. An account cannot be opened with a zero balance and the minimum cannot be paid later in instalments.

The opening deposit may be made in one of two ways:
- Cash in the account currency, counted and confirmed in front of the customer.
- A transfer from another account of the customer, converted at the treasury rate on the day of the transaction.

If the customer deposits a different currency from the one chosen for the account, the converted amount must still reach 100 units of the account currency after conversion. Employees should check the converted amount on the screen before confirming the opening.

## Account opening steps
1. Verify the customer identity using an original national ID or passport.
2. Confirm that the customer has an active EGP account and record the customer reference, for example CUS-123456.
3. Ask the customer to choose one currency. A customer who wants several currencies opens a separate account for each.
4. Take the opening deposit and confirm it meets the minimum.
5. Obtain the customer signature on the account opening form and give the customer a copy.
6. Record the opening as an account service request when the system asks for one.

Each currency is held in its own account, and the Bank does not allow mixing currencies in a single account. The customer receives a statement for each account separately.

## Withdrawals
Cash withdrawals from foreign currency accounts above USD 10,000 or equivalent require 48 hours notice to the branch. The equivalent amount is calculated using the treasury rate on the day the customer gives notice. The notice period allows the branch to obtain enough banknotes in the requested currency.

Withdrawals of USD 10,000 or less, or the equivalent in euros or pounds sterling, can normally be paid on the day, subject to cash availability in the branch.

## Handling a large withdrawal request
When a customer asks for a large cash withdrawal, the employee should follow these steps:
1. Verify the customer identity and check that the balance covers the requested amount.
2. Calculate the equivalent in US dollars. If it is above USD 10,000, explain that 48 hours notice is required.
3. Record the notice with the customer reference, the currency, the amount, and the date and time the notice was given.
4. Tell the customer the earliest date and time when the cash can be collected, which is 48 hours after the notice was recorded.
5. Remind the customer to bring the same identity document on the collection day.

If the customer cannot wait, the employee may offer alternatives such as a bank transfer to another account, a withdrawal in several smaller amounts over separate days, or a draft issued by the Bank. The employee must not split one large withdrawal into smaller same-day payments to avoid the notice requirement.

## Deposits
Cash deposits and transfers into a foreign currency account are accepted during normal branch hours. Deposits are credited on the day of receipt when they are made before the daily cut-off time published in the branch. Employees must count banknotes in front of the customer and reject damaged or suspected counterfeit notes according to the cash handling guideline.

## Reporting
Large cash movements are reported to the compliance unit under the Bank's internal monitoring rules. Employees must not tell the customer that a report has been made, and must not discuss monitoring thresholds with customers.

## Statements and enquiries
Customers receive a monthly statement for each foreign currency account. A customer may ask for a printed statement at the branch after identity verification. Statements are shown in the account currency, and the employee can add the EGP equivalent on request at the treasury rate of the statement date.

When answering balance enquiries, employees may state the balance only to the verified account holder. Never read a balance aloud to a third party, even if the person says they act for the customer.

## Dormant accounts
A foreign currency account with no customer-initiated transaction for 12 months is marked as dormant. Employees must tell a customer with a dormant account that a reactivation request is needed before any withdrawal. The customer must be present with an original identity document, and the request is submitted as an account service request.

## Closing an account
1. Verify the customer identity.
2. Confirm that there are no pending transactions or holds on the account.
3. Pay out the remaining balance in cash in the account currency or transfer it to another account of the customer. Cash payment of a large balance follows the 48 hours notice rule described on the previous page.
4. Record the closure as an account service request and keep the signed closure form.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Opening an account with less than the minimum of 100 units of the account currency.
- Promising same-day cash for a withdrawal above USD 10,000 or equivalent.
- Writing full account numbers in free-text notes.
- Quoting an exchange rate that is not the current treasury rate.
- Offering a currency other than USD, EUR or GBP.

## Where to get help
Questions about this document should be sent to the Retail Accounts Department through the branch supervisor. Treasury rate questions go to the treasury unit. Suspected fraud must be reported immediately to the branch manager.
