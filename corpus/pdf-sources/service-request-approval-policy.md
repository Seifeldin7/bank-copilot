# Service Request Approval Policy
Synthetic training material. Not an official policy of any bank.
Document owner: Branch Operations and Governance Department
Version: 2
Effective date: 2026-01-15
## Purpose and scope
This policy sets out how service requests raised in branches of the Bank must be approved and executed. A service request is a formal record asking for a change or action for a customer, for example ordering a replacement card, resetting a digital banking profile, issuing a cheque book or statement, or asking a customer for missing documents.

Version 2 replaces version 1 of this policy. From the effective date, version 1 is superseded and must not be used. The purpose of the change is to give every request the same independent check by a supervisor before anything happens, so that mistakes and misuse are caught early and every decision can be traced.

The policy applies to all branch employees, supervisors and branch managers, and to any tool used to prepare requests.

## Request categories
Every request belongs to exactly one of four categories:
1. Card service, such as card replacement or a limit enquiry.
2. Digital banking, such as password reset, device change or channel enrolment.
3. Account service, such as cheque books, statements or account detail changes.
4. Additional documentation, such as asking a customer for missing documents by a due date.

The category decides which details must be recorded, but the approval rules in this policy are the same for all categories.

## Definitions
- Employee: any member of branch staff who prepares and submits requests.
- Supervisor: a team leader appointed by the branch manager who is authorised to approve requests.
- Request history: the permanent log of every status change, edit, comment and decision on a request.
- Execution record: the record showing what action was carried out, when, and by whom.

## General rules
- Record the customer reference, for example CUS-123456, and the last four digits of any card or account. Never record full numbers.
- Verify the customer identity before preparing a request.
- Do not take any action for the customer until the request is approved.

## Approval authority
All service requests must be approved by a supervisor before any action is executed. Employees may prepare and submit requests but may never approve them. A supervisor may not approve a request they submitted themselves.

These rules apply to every category, including requests that used to be approved by a senior employee under version 1. Senior status no longer gives approval authority. Only an appointed supervisor can approve.

## Independent approval
The person who prepares a request and the person who approves it must be different people. If a supervisor needs a request for a customer, the supervisor prepares and submits it, and another supervisor approves it. When no second supervisor is present, the request waits, or the branch manager appoints another supervisor to approve it.

The system blocks self-approval, but employees must not try to avoid the rule by sharing credentials or asking a colleague to act in their name. Sharing credentials is a serious breach of the code of conduct.

## Who does what
1. The employee prepares the request, checks the validation results and submits it.
2. The supervisor reviews and either approves or rejects it.
3. After approval, the request is executed and the execution record is stored.

## Urgent requests
Urgency does not remove the approval step. For urgent cases, such as a replacement for a stolen card, the employee must tell the supervisor in person so the request is reviewed quickly. The customer should be told that the action will start only after approval.

## Escalation of delays
If a request stays in Pending approval for more than one working day, the employee informs the branch manager, who makes sure a supervisor reviews it.

## Request lifecycle
Every request has one status at a time, and the status changes only in the order below:
- Draft: the employee is preparing the request and may edit it freely.
- Pending approval: the employee has submitted the request and it waits for a supervisor.
- Approved: a supervisor has approved it and it is ready to execute.
- Rejected: a supervisor has rejected it. A rejected request is final and cannot be executed.
- Executed: the approved action has been carried out.

A Draft can only move to Pending approval. Pending approval can move to Approved or Rejected. Approved can move only to Executed. Rejected and Executed are final.

## Rejection and edits
A supervisor who rejects a request must record a reason. A supervisor may correct a request before approving it; every change is recorded in the request history together with the supervisor identity and timestamp.

A rejection without a reason is not accepted by the system. The reason should be clear enough for the employee to understand what must change, for example "Delivery branch is wrong" or "Customer reference does not match the identity checked". If the employee still wants the action, the employee prepares a new request.

## What supervisors must check
Before approving, the supervisor must review the full request and the validation results shown with it:
- All errors must be resolved. A request with errors cannot be submitted.
- Warnings need a decision. A warning does not stop approval, but the supervisor must read it and be satisfied that it is understood. Examples include a stolen card without a police report reference, a lost card where blocking must be confirmed, a statement period longer than 12 months, or documents requested with a due date more than 14 days away.
- The customer reference, category details and description agree with each other.
- The description explains why the action is needed.

If the supervisor is not satisfied, the supervisor rejects the request with a reason, or corrects it where the fix is clear.

## Execution
An approved request is executed once. Any repeated execution attempt must return the original execution record and must not repeat the action.

This protects customers from duplicates, such as two replacement cards or two fees. If an employee clicks execute twice, or two employees try at the same time, the system shows the first execution record and does nothing else. Employees must not create a second request to repeat an action that was already executed unless the customer has a genuinely new need.

## Recording execution
The execution record contains:
- The date and time of execution.
- The employee who executed the request.
- The action taken and any reference produced, such as a card order reference or ticket reference.
- The link to the approved request.

Execution may happen only after the request shows Approved. An employee must not carry out the action in another system first and register it later.

## Copilot and AI tools
The Bank's copilot and other AI tools may only prepare drafts. They can never submit, approve or execute requests. A draft prepared by a tool is reviewed by the employee, who checks every field against what the customer said and the identity documents. The employee, not the tool, is responsible for the content once it is submitted.

AI tools must not be used by a supervisor in place of reviewing the request, and must never be given the ability to change the status of a request.

## Handling errors in executed requests
If an executed request turns out to be wrong, the employee informs the supervisor. A correction is made by a new request that goes through the full approval process. The executed request is never edited or deleted.

## Records and review
The request history, approvals, rejections and execution records are kept for at least five years. They may be reviewed by the branch manager, internal audit and the compliance unit. Each month the branch manager samples requests and checks that:
- Every executed request had a supervisor approval before execution.
- No supervisor approved a request they submitted.
- Rejections include a reason.
- Edits made by supervisors are recorded with identity and timestamp.
- No action was executed twice.

## Responsibilities
- Employees prepare accurate requests and never approve.
- Supervisors review carefully, give reasons for rejection and approve only what they are satisfied with.
- Branch managers appoint supervisors and make sure one is available during opening hours.
- The Branch Operations and Governance Department owns this policy.

## Changes from version 1
Version 2 changes the approval rules in the following ways:
1. Version 1 allowed any senior employee to approve service requests. Version 2 requires supervisor approval for all requests.
2. Version 1 required supervisor approval only for card replacements. Version 2 applies it to every category.
3. Version 1 did not stop supervisors approving their own requests. Version 2 forbids it.
4. Version 1 treated rejection reasons and edit notes as encouraged. Version 2 requires a recorded reason and a full change history.
5. Version 1 did not address repeated execution. Version 2 requires one execution and the return of the original record.
6. Version 2 adds the rules for the copilot and AI tools.

Employees who learned the old rules must follow version 2 from the effective date. Questions should be sent to the Branch Operations and Governance Department through the branch manager.
