## Digital updates

From the third quarter, internet banking enrollment forms can be printed directly from the branch portal. The new form includes the customer consent section.

Please note the points below when you serve customers who ask about digital banking during the quarter.

1. Use only the new form from the branch portal. Older printed copies in the drawer should be returned to the supervisor and not given to customers.
2. The customer consent section must be read and signed by the customer. Do not tick or sign it for them. A form without the signed consent section must not be attached to a request.
3. The rest of the enrollment process does not change. Any customer with an active current or savings account and a registered mobile number may enroll. The employee verifies the original national ID and creates the digital banking service request.
4. Supervisor approval is still required for all service requests before execution. The activation code is sent by SMS to the registered mobile number only after approval.
5. Activation codes still expire after 24 hours. If the code expires, a new code must be requested at the branch or through the call centre.
6. The mobile app still locks after five failed attempts, and a branch employee must raise a digital banking request to unlock the profile.

If the form does not load, tell the supervisor, who will report it to the digital banking help desk. Please do not use a form saved on a personal device.

Training: a short session on the new form will be held at each branch in the first month of the quarter. Ask the supervisor for the date.

Thank you for your help with a smooth rollout.
