## Statements

Customers may request account statements for any period up to 12 months per request. Statements older than 5 years are retrieved from the archive and take up to 10 working days.

Statement requests are account service requests. The employee prepares the request, and a supervisor must approve it before the statement is produced, because supervisor approval is required for all service requests before execution.

Steps for a standard statement:

1. Verify the identity of the customer with an original national ID or passport.
2. Confirm the customer is the account holder, or an authorised joint holder who is present.
3. Ask for the exact start and end dates. Both dates are required, and the end date cannot be before the start date.
4. Check the period. If it is longer than 12 months, explain that statements are limited to 12 months per request, and split the need into separate requests or shorten the period. The system shows a warning for longer periods.
5. Create the account service request with the customer reference, for example CUS-519047, the account type, and the last 4 digits of the account only.
6. Submit the request for supervisor approval.

Archived statements:

- Statements for periods older than 5 years are not available on screen. They are retrieved from the archive.
- Retrieval takes up to 10 working days, and a fee of EGP 100 per request applies.
- Tell the customer the fee before submitting the request, and note that it was explained.

Example: a customer needs the last two years of statements for a loan application. The employee raises two requests of 12 months each, instead of a single 24 month request.

Never print a statement and leave it unattended at the counter. Hand it directly to the verified customer.

## Balance certificates

A balance certificate is issued on official letterhead and is typically used for embassy or visa applications. Certificates are ready the next working day and carry a fee listed in the retail fee schedule.

The fee for a balance certificate is EGP 25 each. It is debited from the account after the request is approved, so check that the account has enough available funds and is not dormant.

Steps to follow:

1. Verify the identity of the customer using an original document.
2. Ask what the certificate is for, whether it is needed in Arabic, English or both, and as of which date the balance should be stated. The employee should not advise on embassy requirements.
3. Confirm the account type and the last 4 digits of the account.
4. Explain the fee of EGP 25 and the next working day timeline.
5. Create the account service request and submit it for supervisor approval.
6. When the certificate is ready, hand it to the verified customer. If someone else collects it, do not release it unless a valid authorisation has been approved by the supervisor.

A certificate states the balance at a point in time. It does not guarantee that the money will remain in the account. Do not give the customer a certificate for another person's account, and do not offer to alter the wording or amounts.

If the customer needs the certificate urgently on the same day, the answer is that certificates are ready the next working day. Do not promise faster service unless the supervisor confirms an exception in writing.

## Fees and turnaround summary

This summary helps the employee give a quick and correct answer. The retail fee schedule remains the reference for fees.

- Statements for any period up to 12 months per request: raised as an account service request, with supervisor approval before execution.
- Archived statements older than 5 years: EGP 100 per request, up to 10 working days.
- Balance certificate: EGP 25 each, ready the next working day.

Common questions and answers:

1. A customer asks for 18 months of statements. Answer: the limit is 12 months per request. Raise two requests, for example one for the first 12 months and one for the remaining 6 months.
2. A customer asks for a 7 year old statement. Answer: it is archived, takes up to 10 working days, and costs EGP 100 per request.
3. A customer asks whether the certificate fee is waived for embassy use. Answer: no. The fee is EGP 25 unless the fee schedule lists an exception.

Escalate to the supervisor when:

- The customer disputes a statement entry. Use the complaints handling procedure and record the complaint on the day it is received.
- The request is for an account that belongs to someone else.
- The customer cannot prove identity.
- A court or authority requests the statement. Such requests follow a separate legal process and must not be handled at the counter.

Record each request under the customer reference, for example CUS-519047. Do not write the full account number in any note.
