# Cheque Book Requests

Synthetic training material. Not an official policy of any bank.

Document owner: Retail Accounts Department
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-04-01

## Eligibility

Cheque books may be issued only for active current accounts with no returned cheques in the last six months. Savings accounts are not eligible for cheque books.

Before accepting a request, check each of the following on the account screen:

1. The account type is current. If the customer holds a savings account, explain that cheque books cannot be issued. The request form will reject any other account type for a cheque book.
2. The account status is active. Dormant, blocked or restricted accounts are not eligible. A dormant account needs reactivation first.
3. There are no returned cheques in the last six months. A returned cheque is one that was presented for payment and refused, for example because of insufficient funds. The period is counted back from the day of the request.
4. The customer is the account holder or an authorised signatory who has been verified in person.

If the customer has a returned cheque within the last six months, do not submit the request. Explain when the customer may apply again, which is once six months have passed since the most recent returned cheque. Do not suggest ways to avoid the rule.

Example: customer CUS-390671 asks for a cheque book on the account ending 5508. The employee finds the account is current and active, but a cheque was returned four months ago. The request is declined and the customer is told to apply again after the six month period.

The standard cheque book has 25 leaves and costs EGP 40. Mention the fee at the start, so the customer is not surprised. The fee is listed in the retail fee schedule under account service fees.

Cheque books are an account service, so a service request with supervisor approval is needed.

## Delivery

Cheque books are printed centrally and delivered to the requested branch within 7 working days. The customer must collect the cheque book in person.

Tell the customer that the 7 working days start after the request has been approved, not on the day of the visit. Weekends and public holidays are not counted.

At the time of the request:

1. Ask which branch the customer wants to collect from, and record the branch code, for example CAI-012.
2. Confirm that the customer understands that collection is in person only.
3. Advise the customer to bring an original national ID or passport on the collection day.

At collection:

1. Verify the identity of the customer against the Bank records, including the photo and signature.
2. Ask the customer to check that the number of leaves is 25 and that the account details printed on the leaves are correct. The printed account number shows only what is needed for payments, and staff must not copy it into notes.
3. Ask the customer to sign the delivery record.
4. Hand over the cheque book only to the verified customer. A relative, driver or colleague cannot collect it unless the supervisor has approved a written authorisation in line with the privacy guidelines.

If the cheque book has not arrived after 7 working days, check the request history, then contact the cheque printing unit through the supervisor. Do not raise a second request for the same cheque book, because an approved request is executed only once.

Uncollected cheque books are returned for safe destruction after the period set by the printing unit. Tell customers to collect soon after they receive the notification.

## Request procedure

Follow these steps to submit a cheque book request:

1. Verify the identity of the customer under the Customer Identification and Verification Standard.
2. Check eligibility using the previous section of this guide.
3. Create an account service request with the service type cheque book, the account type current, and the last 4 digits of the account. Enter the customer reference, for example CUS-390671.
4. Add a description that includes the number of leaves, the fee of EGP 40 explained, and the collection branch.
5. Submit the request for supervisor approval. Supervisor approval is required for all service requests before execution.
6. After approval and execution, give the customer the service reference number and repeat the delivery timeline of up to 7 working days.

The supervisor can reject the request, and must record a reason. For example, the supervisor may reject it because a returned cheque was missed in the first check. The supervisor can also correct details such as the delivery branch before approving, and each change is recorded in the request history.

The fee of EGP 40 is debited from the current account. If the available balance is lower than the fee, tell the customer before submitting and let the customer decide whether to proceed.

Lost or stolen cheque books are not handled in this guide. If a customer reports one, tell the supervisor immediately so that the remaining cheques can be stopped and the account protected.
