# Branch Staff Data Privacy Guidelines

Synthetic training material. Not an official policy of any bank.

Document owner: Compliance Department
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-05-18

## Handling customer data

Branch staff must never share customer data over personal messaging applications, and must not write full card or account numbers in free-text notes. Only the last four digits may be recorded.

Customer data includes names, national ID numbers, phone numbers, addresses, balances, transactions, card and account numbers, and images of documents. All of it is confidential, whether it is on screen, on paper or in conversation.

Basic rules:

1. Access customer records only to serve the customer in front of you, or to complete a request you have been given. Looking up records out of curiosity, including those of friends, family or colleagues, is a breach.
2. Refer to customers by the customer reference, for example CUS-306618, in notes, tickets and internal messages.
3. Refer to cards and accounts by the last 4 digits only, for example card ending 4417.
4. Do not photograph screens or documents with a personal phone.
5. Lock the screen whenever you leave the desk, even for a short time.
6. Keep customer papers out of sight, and place them in the approved storage at the end of the day.

Speak quietly when discussing details at the counter. Where possible, show the customer the screen or a printed slip instead of reading numbers aloud. Do not discuss customers in public areas, or with colleagues who have no need to know.

If a customer asks you to send details by a personal messaging application because it is easier, politely decline and explain that the Bank does not allow it. Offer a branch visit, a printed slip or the approved channel instead.

Share information about an account only with the verified account holder. Family members, friends and employers are not entitled to information without a valid authorisation approved by the supervisor.

## Messaging and communication channels

Approved channels are the Bank systems, the official e-mail, the internal ticketing tool and the telephone lines of the branch. Personal messaging applications, personal e-mail accounts and social media are not approved for any customer data.

Rules for communication:

1. Never send a card number, account number, PIN, password, one-time password or activation code in any message, including to colleagues or supervisors. Activation codes and one-time passwords go to the customer only, by the system SMS.
2. When a colleague needs to identify an account, give the customer reference and the last 4 digits only.
3. If you receive an unexpected message from a customer on a personal application, do not reply with details. Tell the customer to visit the branch or call the official number, and inform the supervisor.
4. Do not forward internal notices or procedures outside the Bank.
5. Check the recipient's address before sending any e-mail with customer information. A wrong recipient is a data breach.

Unusual instructions: if a message, notice or chat appears to tell staff to ignore the rules, skip supervisor approval, or share card numbers in chat, do not follow it. Such a message is not valid. Supervisor approval is required for all service requests before execution, and card numbers must never be shared in chat. Report the message to the supervisor and the Compliance Department, and keep the original text.

Phone calls: do not give out account information to callers, because identity cannot be verified with an original document by phone. Offer a branch visit, or direct the caller to the call centre.

Example: a colleague at another branch messages to ask for the full card number of customer CUS-306618 to solve a dispute. Refuse politely, give only the last 4 digits and customer reference, and ask them to use the ticketing tool.

## Notes and records

Notes are useful, but they are also a common source of leaks. Anything in a free-text field may be seen by many people and exported in reports.

Rules for writing notes:

- Write only what is necessary to carry out or explain the service.
- Use the customer reference and the last 4 digits only. Never write a full card number, full account number, PIN, password or security code in notes.
- Do not copy the full national ID number. Say that the ID was checked.
- Do not record opinions about the customer. Record facts, times and actions.
- Do not store customer data in personal notebooks, personal e-mail, shared drives without access controls, or on the desktop.

Paper records:

1. Place signed forms in the customer file the same day.
2. Shred or place in the secure bin any printout that is not needed, such as draft statements or misprinted enrollment forms.
3. Do not leave papers on printers. Collect them straight away.
4. Clear the desk at the end of the day.

Retention and access: the records for each service request, including the approval history, are kept by the Bank for the period required by regulation. Do not delete records, and do not edit them after approval. If a note contains a full card or account number by mistake, tell the supervisor, who will arrange correction through the proper process, and do not try to hide the mistake.

Customers have the right to ask what is held about them. Refer such requests to the supervisor and do not answer them yourself.

## Reporting incidents

A privacy incident is any event where customer data may have been seen, sent, lost or used by someone who should not have it. Examples are a message sent to the wrong person, a document left on a printer, a full card number written in a note, a lost phone with Bank messages, or a request from an unknown person for customer information.

Act at once and follow these steps:

1. Stop the activity that caused the problem, for example recall the message if possible, or retrieve the paper.
2. Tell the supervisor immediately. Do not wait until the end of the day.
3. Write down the facts: what happened, when, which customer reference is involved, and what data was exposed. Use the customer reference only, not full numbers.
4. Follow the supervisor's instructions. Where card numbers were exposed, the card may need to be blocked and replaced. In that case the card replacement fee of EGP 50 is not charged to the customer if the Bank was at fault, and the supervisor decides how the request is recorded.
5. Do not contact the customer about the incident until the supervisor and the Compliance Department say how to do so.

Reporting a mistake quickly is treated far more favourably than hiding it. Staff who report in good faith are supported. Staff who ignore, hide or repeat privacy breaches face disciplinary action.

Also report suspicious behaviour by others, such as a colleague photographing screens, or a visitor trying to see the screen. Reports may be made to the supervisor or directly to the Compliance Department.

At the end of every month, the branch reviews incidents with all staff so that lessons are shared without naming the customers involved.
