# Branch ATM Cash Withdrawal Guidelines

Synthetic training material. Not an official policy of any bank.

## Daily withdrawal limits

Classic debit cards may withdraw up to EGP 20,000 per day from branch ATMs. Platinum debit cards may withdraw up to EGP 50,000 per day. Limits reset at midnight Cairo time.

## Captured cards

If an ATM captures a customer's card, the customer can collect it from the branch within 3 working days with a valid national ID. Cards not collected within 3 working days are destroyed and a replacement must be requested.

## Cash not dispensed

If the ATM debits the account but does not dispense cash, the employee records a complaint with the ATM location and time. The amount is reversed within 7 working days after reconciliation.
